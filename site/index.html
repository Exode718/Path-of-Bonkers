Wraeclast Conversion total conversion for Megabonk

Wraeclast on Megabonk's bones.

Seven Path of Exile classes, each with one signature skill and ten upgrades. 79 relics and 30 tomes replace Megabonk's player layer; the world, terrain, enemies and UI shell stay. Three floors, a one-minute boss window, then an endless finale.

Windows only, Megabonk on Steam. The installer backs up the loader files and can undo itself. Builds are private while the mod ships Path of Exile art.

The ten upgrades of each skill

Every upgrade can stack; the card states its cost.

Marauder — Righteous Fire 10 upgrades
  • Fan the Flames — The aura's radius is 8% bigger per stack for every burning enemy inside, up to 40% per stack. Its burn deals 10% less.
  • Pyre of the Faithful — Standing still for 1 second: the aura deals 80% more damage per stack. Its radius is 15% smaller and moving cancels the bonus.
  • Cinder Trail — Every aura tick burns the ground behind you for 1.5 s: 25% of the aura's damage per stack every 0.5 s. The aura itself deals 10% less.
  • Sacrificial Pyre — While an enemy burns you lose 1.5% maximum Life/s; each kill inside heals 1% (max 4%/s). Off below 15% Life.
  • Immolation — A burning enemy that dies explodes for 25% of its maximum Life in 3 m; the blast cannot ignite.
  • Zealot's Covenant — The aura slows enemies 20%. Its burn deals 20% less.
  • Ashen Veil — With 3+ enemies burning, you take 10% less damage. The aura is 15% smaller.
  • Kindle — Burning enemies take +6% more damage per second burning, up to +30%. The aura's burn deals 15% less.
  • Smouldering Step — A dash leaves burning ground for 2 s. Your skill cooldown is 30% longer.
  • Last Rites — Under 30% Life the aura deals double damage for 4 s (once per 20 s) and you do not regenerate.
Ranger — Ice Shot 10 upgrades
  • Ricochet — The arrow bounces to the nearest enemy once per stack for 60% of the damage that landed, and it no longer pierces.
  • Volley Stance — The shot fires 3 arrows per stack in a 20 degree spread per stack; each arrow deals 65% and pierces nothing.
  • Glacial Lance — Each enemy pierced adds 30% damage per stack; the shot's cooldown is 15% longer.
  • Shatterpoint — Hits on a frozen enemy deal +50% and burst into 3 shards at 20%. One shatter per freeze.
  • Winter's Grasp — Where the arrow expires it leaves a 2 s frost patch that slows 40%. The patch deals no damage.
  • Hunter's Mark — Marks the farthest enemy; hits on it splash 25% damage in 2 m. One mark at a time.
  • Frostbite — Each hit on a frozen enemy adds +8% freeze chance. The arrow deals 15% less damage.
  • Sleet Step — +10% Movement Speed for 2 s after a hit. No damage bonus.
  • Ice Prison — Every 3rd hit on the same enemy roots it 1 s. The first two hits cannot freeze.
  • Cold Snap — A frozen enemy that dies freezes enemies within 3 m for 0.5 s. Shatter damage is 30% lower.
Witch — Fireball 10 upgrades
  • Twin Flame — Each cast adds one more ball per stack at a random angle for 35% of the shot's damage; the main ball deals 15% less.
  • Split Ash — On impact the ball splits into 2 more balls per stack, at 30 then 60 degrees, for 45% damage; every shot has a 25% smaller impact radius.
  • Pyromantic Focus — The cast is 20% slower and deals 60% more damage per stack, with 25% more impact radius per stack.
  • Rolling Ember — The ball bounces once per pick; the bounced hit does not ignite.
  • Ignite Bloom — An ignited enemy that dies explodes for 50% of the hit that killed it; only while the ignite runs.
  • Ember Familiar — Each stack adds an ember orbiting you; the embers take turns lobbing a 40% fireball every 3 s. Your own casts deal 10% less.
  • Meteor Call — Fireballs fall from the sky after 0.8 s: 1.2x damage in a 1.4x radius, growing with each pick. Each extra projectile adds its own impact point 4 m away; the spare balls are only animation.
  • Wildfire — The ignite spreads to one enemy within 2.5 m every second. Ignite damage is 20% lower.
  • Ash Cloud — The impact leaves a ground smoke cloud and you take 20% less damage inside. Each pick makes it longer, larger and stronger (cap 8 s / 60% / 11 m). The cloud deals no damage.
  • Combustion — Each re-hit on an ignited enemy adds +15% damage; the bonus and the hit cap grow with every pick. It cannot refresh the ignite.
Duelist — Cyclone 10 upgrades
  • Gale Offshoots — Every 3rd spin hurls a small cyclone in a random direction: 4 m, 40% damage, pierces. Each stack adds one cyclone; Cyclone damage is 15% lower (paid once).
  • Vortex Lure — Enemies inside 1.6x the radius are dragged 30% closer each spin; every further stack adds 15% (max 5 m). The radius is 10% smaller (paid once).
  • Gathering Storm — Replaces the spin: charge for 2.0 s while standing still (moving restarts it), then release the storm at 2.2x radius and 3x damage. A second stack charges in 1.5 s and releases at 3.6x.
  • Razorwind — The spin leaves a blade trail behind you: 1.5 s, 50% of the spin's damage every 0.5 s. One patch per metre.
  • Momentum — +2% spin damage per enemy hit, up to +20%. Resets 2 s after the last hit.
  • Iron Cyclone — The spin is 25% wider and deals 15% more damage; you move 30% slower while it runs.
  • Twin Orbit — Two blades orbit at 1.2x radius for 40% damage; the inner 40% of the radius is never hit.
  • Blood Whirl — Critical hits inside the spin heal 1% of maximum Life. Critical chance is 20% lower.
  • Vacuum Cut — Every 5th spin knocks enemies back 2 m; that ring deals no damage.
  • Afterimage — When no enemy is left in the spin it leaves a ghost cyclone for 1.5 s at half radius and damage; 1 s before it can return.
Templar — Ice Nova 10 upgrades
  • Glacial Echo — The nova repeats 0.6 s later, once per stack, at 50% of its damage. The echo cannot freeze.
  • Convergence — Hits at the edge of the nova deal 40% more damage per stack and drag enemies 2 m toward the centre. Centre hits are unchanged.
  • Deep Freeze — The nova's freeze lasts 50% longer per stack. Its cooldown is 20% longer.
  • Frozen Bulwark — A 2 s ice field forms at the burst's edge and slows enemies 40%. It deals no damage.
  • Permafrost — The ground inside the ring stays frozen 3 s and slows 40%. The nova deals 10% less damage.
  • Rime Cascade — Hitting 3+ enemies refunds 30% of the cooldown. Single-target casts give nothing.
  • Ring of Winter — The ring is 25% wider but deals 25% less damage.
  • Fracture — Enemies killed by the nova burst into 3 ice shards. The shards cannot freeze.
  • Icebound — Enemies are rooted 0.4 s instead of frozen. No slow after the root.
  • Arctic Charge — Every 3rd cast charges for 1 s, then bursts 40% wider.
Shadow — Viper Strike of the Mamba 10 upgrades
  • Toxic Bloom — A poisoned enemy that dies leaves a cloud for 3 s: 50% of the strike's damage per stack every 0.5 s within 3 m.
  • Venom Spread — When a poisoned enemy dies its poison jumps to one enemy within 4 m per stack. A corpse jumps only once.
  • Serpent's Patience — Standing still for 2 s: the next strike deals 50% more damage per stack and adds 2 poison stacks per stack. Moving resets the timer.
  • Shadowstep — Viper Strike deals +35% damage and adds one poison stack. Every further stack adds +35% and one more stack.
  • Corrosive Bite — Poisoned enemies take +10% damage per stack, up to +30%.
  • Twin Fang — The strike hits twice for 65% each. The first hit cannot poison.
  • Lethal Dose — Poison deals +25% more damage per stack, but lasts 35% less time (paid once).
  • Adder's Grace — +15% Movement Speed while an enemy is poisoned. The strike deals 10% less damage.
  • Haemorrhage — Poisoned enemies drop 1 extra gold.
  • Last Breath — A kill on a poisoned enemy refunds the strike's cooldown.
Scion — Arc 10 upgrades
  • Static Field — Every jump leaves a spark zone for 1.5 s: 25% of the jump's damage per stack every 0.5 s within 2 m. The chain has one jump fewer.
  • Overcharge — The chain gains 2 jumps per stack, but each jump loses 25% instead of 18%.
  • Storm Anchor — The last enemy hit is stunned for 0.6 s per stack and takes a second hit 0.4 s later for the last jump's damage per stack.
  • Forked Fate — The cast throws two bolts at once, each with half the jumps.
  • Conductivity — Hitting a stunned enemy spreads the stun to 2 neighbours. Stun lasts 25% less time.
  • Chain Lightning — Jumps reach 8 m and prefer an unhit enemy, but each jump loses an extra 10% damage.
  • Thunderhead — Every 4th cast calls a bolt on the densest pack for 1.5x damage. Cooldown is 10% longer.
  • Static Cling — Stunned enemies take +12% damage from everything. The first hit of a chain cannot stun.
  • Galvanic Rush — +10% Movement Speed for 1.5 s after a chain ends. Chain damage is 10% lower.
  • Ball Lightning — The bolt becomes a slow orb that zaps for 2 s; no chain jumps.

Run structure

Three floors, then an endless finale. You can summon the boss early and leave before the timer ends, but there is no bonus for it.

FloorTimerThen
110:00Boss + a one-minute ghost wave. Kill the boss inside that minute to reach floor 2; otherwise you die.
29:00Same boss + one-minute rule.
38:00No boss; the portal opens automatically.
Final—Kill the final boss; the endless wave begins.

Install

One folder, one script. The installer finds the game, checks the build, backs up what it replaces and copies the mod in. Close Megabonk first.

  1. Download the release archive

    Use the download button above (or grab WraeclastConversion-v0.9.215.zip from Releases) and unpack it anywhere outside the game folder. It contains the loader, the plugin and the installer.

  2. Run the installer

    Double-click install.cmd (it asks for elevation), or run the script directly: 

    powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\install.ps1

    If Megabonk is not in a standard Steam library, the installer asks for the folder. -DryRun shows what it would do. The installer refuses a second install while a backup exists.

  3. Launch Megabonk normally

    The mod loads with the game. To check it, open BepInEx\LogOutput.log and look for Wraeclast Conversion 0.9.215.

  4. Update or undo

    An update is an uninstall followed by an install. Double-click uninstall.cmd, or: 

    powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\uninstall.ps1

    The backup in _wraeclast_backup is restored and removed. Saves are never touched.
